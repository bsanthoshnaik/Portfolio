SANThOSH NAIK PORTFOLIO — QUICK START

1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open the extracted folder in VS Code.
3. Open index.html in your browser (or use the Live Server extension).
4. Replace assets/profile.svg with your own photo:
   - Put your photo in the assets folder, for example santhosh-photo.jpg.
   - In index.html, change src="assets/profile.svg" to src="assets/santhosh-photo.jpg" for both photo images.
5. Add your resume:
   - Put your resume PDF in this folder and name it resume.pdf.
   - The Download Resume and Watch Resume buttons already point to resume.pdf.
6. Update your actual profile links in index.html:
   - Search for https://www.linkedin.com/ and replace with your LinkedIn profile URL.
   - Search for https://github.com/ and replace with your GitHub profile URL.
   - Search for https://leetcode.com/ and replace with your LeetCode profile URL.
7. Update your phone number:
   - In index.html, replace "+910000000000" and "Add your phone number" with your real number.
8. Check the education section and add your college names, dates, and marks.
9. For deployment, upload the files to GitHub Pages, Netlify, or another static hosting service.

Files:
- index.html: page content
- style.css: desktop/mobile design and responsive layout
- script.js: mobile menu and Hire Me popup
- assets/profile.svg: temporary photo placeholder

IMPORTANT:
- The LinkedIn, GitHub, and LeetCode links are examples until you replace them.
- The phone number is intentionally a placeholder.
- The resume buttons work after you add resume.pdf.
